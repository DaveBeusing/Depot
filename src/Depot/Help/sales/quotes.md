# Sales Quotes

Quotes live in the **Sales > Overview > Quotes** area of the Commercial Hub.

## Workflow

1. Create a new quote and select the customer.
2. Select the commercial contact when available.
3. Add items. Depot resolves each item through Customer → Region → Global and records the selected price-list source and scope.
4. Save the draft and generate a Quote PDF when required.
5. Use **Email quote** to open a local mail draft with the generated PDF attached.
6. Mark the quote as sent.
7. Record it as accepted or rejected.
8. Convert an accepted or still-valid sent quote into a Sales Order draft.

Automatically sourced draft lines are refreshed when a quote draft is saved; manual values are retained. The conversion keeps customer, address, pricing source, discount, tax and item snapshots from the accepted quote. It does not reprice the quote during conversion. The resulting Sales Order continues through the normal approval, reservation, shipping and invoicing workflow.

Expired quotes cannot be converted. Posted Sales activity remains auditable rather than being edited in place.
