# Sales Leads

Use **Sales > Leads** to capture potential customers before they become Customer master records.

## Capture a lead

A Lead can contain a company name, person name, email, phone, source, owner, status and notes summary. The list is searched and paged through the CRM service rather than loading the entire lead population.

## Activities

For a selected Lead you can schedule calls, emails, meetings or follow-ups. Planned Activities retain due time, owner, subject and notes. Completion and cancellation are explicit state changes and retain evidence. Due and overdue owned Activities can also appear in **My Work**.

## Convert a lead

Use **Convert** to qualify the Lead into the ERP sales flow. Conversion can either link an existing Customer or create a Customer through the existing Customer service and then creates the initial Opportunity.

The conversion stores the resulting Customer and Opportunity links. A converted Lead cannot be converted a second time.

## Permissions

SalesCrm.View controls visibility. Record mutation additionally requires CRM record-management authority. Activity visibility and mutation use their dedicated CRM Activity permissions. Authorization is enforced in services even if a UI action is hidden.
