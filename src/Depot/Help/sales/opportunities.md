# Sales Opportunities

Use **Sales > Opportunities** to manage qualified commercial work against an existing Customer.

## Pipeline fields

An Opportunity records its owner, stage, expected close date, currency, expected amount, user-maintained probability, optional next-activity date and outcome. The pipeline summary groups open Opportunities by configured stage and calculates expected and probability-weighted amounts in the database.

## Stages and follow-up

Stages are ordered and can be activated/deactivated by authorized CRM managers. Activities can be scheduled against an Opportunity. Owned Opportunities with a missing or due next activity can appear in **My Work**.

## Create a quote

**Create Quote** creates a draft through the existing Sales Quote service. Depot stores the created Quote link on the Opportunity and retains the Opportunity number as source reference. A second linked Quote cannot be created through this conversion action.

## Close an opportunity

Open Opportunities can be closed as **Won** or **Lost**. A close reason is required. Closing an Opportunity is commercial pipeline evidence only; it does not post revenue or accounting entries.

## Permissions

SalesCrm.View controls visibility. Record mutation requires CRM record-management authority. CRM management authority controls stage configuration and broader ownership operations. Activity permissions remain separate.
